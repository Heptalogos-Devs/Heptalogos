# Life and Agency

Life owns adopted Product Activities and useful continuation facts. Agency owns exact native delivery adoption, the persisted send point, and real per-part outcomes. Nous owns cognitive Subject identity and cognition; OpenClaw owns native model/tool execution and durable network delivery.

Keep current account, actor, Activity, original sourceIndex, reply/thread facts and source permission explicit. Serialize cancellation and a new send point on the same Activity row. Known accepted results remain readable after cancellation; an uncertain attempt never authorizes automatic replay. Never add a model loop or network retry worker here.

Mutations use the existing Host-fenced Persistence transaction. Tests for actual locking, recovery and outcome preservation live in integration/foundation. The canonical schemas generate HTTP/Client contracts; do not duplicate their DTOs.
